Twin transactions

The Twin type on the New page saves one purchase as two rows: a transfer from the paying account to the To account (e.g. Cash), with notes from this pattern, and a withdrawal with the real payee in the expense account (e.g. Household).

Exchange rates: extra currencies

Currencies used by an open account always get month-end rates in MMEX. Tick others to give them rates too, e.g. one you'll travel in. Ticking fills their past month-ends from the xe.com tables already saved, with no visit to xe.com; later downloads include them. Unticking stops new ones and keeps what's stored.{% if currencies | selectattr('on_xe', 'false') | list %} Marked "not on xe.com": missing from xe.com's latest saved table (usually a retired currency), so nothing fills it.{% endif %}
